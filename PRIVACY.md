# Privacy Policy: hope-post

*Last updated: November 11, 2026*

**hope-post** is a personal desktop tool. It runs only on the account owner's own computer and is used only with the
owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube and other platforms the owner connects).

## What it does
- Uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts (for example, as TikTok drafts or YouTube uploads).
- Reads basic profile info and statistics of the owner's own videos so the owner can track how they perform.

## What it collects
- Nothing from anyone else. It does not collect, store, sell or share data about other users or viewers.
- Login access tokens for the owner's accounts are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any
  third party other than the platform they belong to (TikTok, Google/YouTube, etc.).

## Sharing
- No data is sold or shared with third parties. There are no ads, analytics trackers or outside servers.

## Your control
- Access can be removed at any time from the platform's settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app
  permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party connections).

## Contact
Questions: open an issue on this repository.
